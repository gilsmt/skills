# Grammar Check

Grammar & Mechanics:

- Fix any grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors
- Identify and correct verb tense inconsistencies
- Fix subject-verb agreement issues

Style & Clarity:

- Suggest improvements for sentence structure and flow
- Identify wordy or unclear phrases and provide concise alternatives
- Flag passive voice where active voice would be stronger
- Point out repetitive language or overused words

Tone & Readability:

- Assess the overall tone and suggest adjustments if needed
- Identify sentences that are too long or complex
- Suggest vocabulary improvements where appropriate
- Ensure consistent voice throughout

Then to finish, before finalizing, write some style suggestions to highlight opportunities for improvement and and overall assessment with Brief comment on strengths and areas for growth. Please be thorough but constructive in your feedback.
